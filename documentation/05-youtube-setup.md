# YouTube channel setup: Ask Ivo

Do these steps yourself, signed in to your own Google account. Nothing here is automated.

## 1. Create the channel
1. Sign in to youtube.com with the Google account that will own the channel. Consider a dedicated account for the brand.
2. Click your profile picture, then **Create a channel**. Choose the option to use a **custom name** (a brand channel), not your personal name.
3. Name: **Ask Ivo**
4. Handle: try **@askivo**. If it's taken, try **@askivo.explains**, **@ask.ivo** or **@askivoexplains**. Tell me which one you get.
5. Verify the account by phone at youtube.com/verify. This unlocks custom thumbnails, uploads longer than 15 minutes and live streaming.

## 4. Channel settings (Settings → Channel)
- **Country of residence:** your actual country.
- **Keywords:** `AI explained, artificial intelligence, how AI works, technology explained, explainer, science explained, economics explained, financial literacy, how things work, Ask Ivo, animated explainer`
- **Audience:** "No, set this channel as not made for kids." The content is aimed at adults.

## 6. Upload Episode 1 (as a Short)
1. In Studio, click **Create**, then **Upload videos**, and choose `renders/ep001-ask-ai/master_clean.mp4`. That's the one without burned-in captions; upload `master_captioned.mp4` instead if you prefer the burned-in look.
2. **Title:** `What Actually Happens When You Ask AI a Question?`
3. **Description:**
```
You ask an AI a question, and seconds later there's an answer. But it isn't pulling a finished reply from a file. Here's what actually happens, step by step, and why fluent isn't the same as correct.

Sources and further reading:
• OpenAI Help: What are tokens? https://help.openai.com/en/articles/4936856-what-are-tokens-and-how-to-count-them
• Google ML Crash Course: Large language models https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course/llm
• Kalai et al. (2025), Why Language Models Hallucinate https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04664
• Lewis et al. (2020), Retrieval-Augmented Generation https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401

Simplified for clarity: token splits and probability bars are illustrative.
Narration voiced with licensed AI text-to-speech. Animation, music and sound are original.

Ask Ivo — Big questions. Simple explanations.
#AI #ChatGPT #HowAIWorks #Explained #Shorts
```
   Consider leaving out #ChatGPT, because the video doesn't name any product.
4. **Audience:** Not made for kids.
5. **Altered or synthetic content:** this is for realistic content that could mislead viewers. An animated robot with a narrator generally doesn't need it. Read YouTube's current help page and record your decision.
6. **Subtitles:** add `renders/ep001-ask-ai/captions.srt` (upload file, "with timing").
7. **Visibility:** start with **Private** or **Unlisted**, watch it on your phone, then publish or schedule.
8. **Cover / thumbnail:** for Shorts, YouTube usually lets you pick a frame from the video in the mobile app. The opening frame was designed to work as the cover.
